Product

Already Delivered

The features that are live in the Socialbridge wallet today — including the Telegram Mini App deployed at app.socialbridgeapp.com.

Security-first vault

Live

Private keys stay on your device inside a sealed vault dedicated to secrets, with a separate app vault for confidential metadata such as account lists and history.

  • Dedicated secret vault plus an app vault for confidential metadata
  • libsodium crypto: Argon2id + XChaCha20-Poly1305
  • Non-custodial by design: we never see seeds or passphrases

TON Connect + multi-account

Live

Connect to TON dApps in one tap and keep multiple profiles ready for swaps, collections, or testing. Each account carries its own jettons and NFTs.

  • Native TON Connect flow without manual address sharing
  • Fast account switching with per-profile preferences
  • Unified asset view spanning TON, jettons, and collectibles

Buy Crypto (fiat on-ramp)

Live

Buy crypto directly from the wallet UI through the Changelly Fiat API. Identity verification (KYC/AML) and payment processing are handled by the provider.

Swaps across TON DEXes

Live

Swap assets using on-chain liquidity from STON.fi, DeDust, and swap.coffee, plus Changelly cross-asset exchange — with quotes and simulation before you sign.

Liquidity & farming (STON.fi)

Live

Provide and remove liquidity and farm on STON.fi. Multi-step actions are orchestrated with clear lifecycle states (prepare → sign → sent → confirmed) and safe recovery guidance.

Staking

Live

Stake and unstake, see staking positions, and review activity history in-wallet.

NFTs & Jettons

Live

View and send jettons and NFT collectibles. (Native NFT minting and an in-wallet marketplace are not shipped yet.)

Pre-flight simulation

Live

Every TON transaction is replayed through a local sandbox virtual machine first, so you see final balances, money paths, and refunds before broadcasting.

Telegram Mini App

Live

The same wallet runs inside Telegram, deployed at app.socialbridgeapp.com.
